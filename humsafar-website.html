Your comfort comes first

Take your time. Keep your boundaries.

A good introduction should feel respectful. Share information at a pace that works for you and involve people you trust when you are ready.

Read common questions

A few good habits

  • Keep early conversations in the app.
  • Do not share passwords, verification codes, or financial details.
  • Take time to confirm important information.
  • Pause or end a conversation whenever you feel uncomfortable.
Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Humsafar a marriage-focused service?

Yes. Humsafar is designed to help people explore introductions with marriage in mind.

Can I choose what appears on my profile?

The app demo lets you edit profile details. A live service should provide clear privacy controls before real member information is collected.

Are the profiles on this website real?

No. Names and profiles shown here are sample content used to demonstrate the design.

Can I use Humsafar on my phone?

Yes. The linked app demo is designed to fit mobile screens as well as desktop browsers.
